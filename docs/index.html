How to read this

  • This is an evolving situation. Findings marked PROVISIONAL, UNVERIFIED or HYPOTHESIS may change.
  • Every load-bearing claim carries a pointer to the section of the private evidence log supporting it, in the form (Z-7), (U-4), (R-1).
  • The evidence tree does not ship. It holds victim identities, suspect-side financial detail, and images belonging to people who never consented. That exclusion is deliberate and the boundary is written down in the redaction contract.
  • Found an error? That is the point. Corrections are recorded with the same care as findings.
